# Decline patterns

Adapt the words; keep the shape.

**Firm no**
> Thanks for thinking of me, <name>. I'm going to pass on this one: <one reason, if given>.
> I hope it goes really well.

**Not now**
> Thanks, <name>. I can't take this on before <date>. If it's still open then, I'd be glad to
> look at it again.

**No, but here is someone who can**
> Thanks for asking. I'm not the right person for this, but <colleague> knows <topic> well. I've
> copied them in. (Only copy someone in if the user named them.)

**Meeting invitation**
> Thanks for the invite. I won't be able to join. Could you share the notes afterwards?

**Vendor or sales pitch**
> Thanks for reaching out. We're not looking at this right now, so I'll pass.
